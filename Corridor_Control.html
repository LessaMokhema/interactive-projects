Corridor Control

A what-if tool for a freight network built around rail, ports and pipelines. Pick a corridor, trigger a disruption, then scrub through the next 14 days to watch flows fall, recover and ripple into neighbouring corridors. All figures are illustrative round numbers, not operating data.
