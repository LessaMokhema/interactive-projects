Prototype by Palesa Mokhema. Draw a circuit and Fahrlinie finds the fastest line, calculates the lap time and drives it. The car is modelled as a point mass with a friction circle: cornering speed comes from curvature and grip, and braking and acceleration are limited by whatever grip is left. The fastest line is found by relaxing the path inside the track edges. Circuits and times are simulated.
